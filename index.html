Inspection Standards

Applied to every production lot and in-stock dispatch.

    Our QC Process

    1. 1

      Greige & Yarn Check

      Yarn count, density and greige fabric inspected before dyeing.

    2. 2

      Lab Dip & Strike-off Approval

      Colour matched under D65 / TL84 light and approved by you before bulk.

    3. 3

      Bulk Testing

      Weight, width, shrinkage, colourfastness and tensile tested per lot.

    4. 4

      Final Roll Inspection & Packing

      Four-point inspection, shade bands, roll labels and packing list sent before shipment.
